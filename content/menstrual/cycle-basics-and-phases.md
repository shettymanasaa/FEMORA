# Menstrual Cycle Basics and Biological Phases

Understanding your menstrual cycle helps you recognize personal baseline patterns, plan activities, and identify potential health changes.

## The Four Biological Phases

### 1. Menstruation Phase (Days 1–5 to 7)
- **Hormonal Profile:** Estrogen and progesterone drop to baseline levels.
- **Physiology:** The uterus sheds its endometrium (functional lining), resulting in menstrual bleeding.
- **Common Sensations:** Mild uterine cramping due to prostaglandin release, lower energy levels, and mild fatigue.

### 2. Follicular Phase (Days 1–13)
- **Hormonal Profile:** The pituitary gland secretes Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH). Ovarian follicles mature, producing increasing amounts of estradiol (estrogen).
- **Physiology:** Rising estrogen rebuilds the endometrial lining, preparing it for possible implantation.
- **Common Sensations:** Rising physical and mental stamina, clearer skin, and improved mood.

### 3. Ovulation Phase (Typically around Day 14 in a 28-day cycle)
- **Hormonal Profile:** High estrogen triggers a surge of Luteinizing Hormone (LH).
- **Physiology:** The dominant follicle ruptures, releasing a mature oocyte (egg) into the fallopian tube.
- **Common Sensations:** Thin, stretchy cervical mucus (egg-white consistency), subtle basal body temperature dip followed by a rise, and occasional mild unilateral lower abdominal twinges (*mittelschmerz*).

### 4. Luteal Phase (Days 15–28)
- **Hormonal Profile:** The ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, producing progesterone and moderate estrogen.
- **Physiology:** Progesterone stabilizes and enriches the uterine lining. If fertilization does not occur, the corpus luteum degenerates, causing hormone levels to decline.
- **Common Sensations:** Fluid retention, breast tenderness, appetite changes, or mood sensitivity during the latter half of this phase.

## Educational Note
Every body is unique. Cycle lengths typically range between 21 and 35 days in healthy adults, with bleeding lasting between 2 and 7 days.
