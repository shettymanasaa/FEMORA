# Sexual Wellbeing and Reproductive Health Education

Sexual wellbeing encompasses physical comfort, emotional boundaries, informed communication, and reproductive agency.

## Hormonal Variations and Libido
- **Ovulatory Window:** Estrogen and luteinizing hormone surges during the late follicular and ovulatory phases naturally enhance pelvic blood flow and often coincide with increased sexual desire and heightened sensitivity.
- **Luteal and Menstrual Phases:** Elevated progesterone in the luteal phase, followed by low hormone levels during menses, can lower libido and alter vaginal moisture levels.
- **Physical Discomfort:** Any persistent pain with intercourse (*dyspareunia*) is not something you must simply tolerate. It can stem from insufficient lubrication, hormonal changes, pelvic floor tension, or conditions such as endometriosis or vulvodynia, and should be discussed with a healthcare provider.

## Safe Practices and Barrier Protection
- **STI Prevention:** Barrier methods (e.g., external and internal condoms, dental dams) provide essential protection against Sexually Transmitted Infections regardless of cycle phase or contraceptive status.
- **Hygiene and Vaginal Flora:** The vagina is self-cleansing and maintains an acidic microenvironment dominated by *Lactobacillus* species. Avoid scented washes, douches, and harsh soaps that disrupt natural pH and predispose to bacterial vaginosis or yeast infections.
