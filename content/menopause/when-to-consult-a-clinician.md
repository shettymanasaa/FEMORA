# When to Consult a Qualified Healthcare Professional for Menopause

While menopausal transition symptoms are part of a natural hormonal shift, certain clinical presentations require dedicated medical investigation.

## Urgent Warning Sign: Postmenopausal Bleeding
- **What It Means:** Any vaginal bleeding, pink discharge, brownish spotting, or staining that occurs **after 12 consecutive months without periods** (or unexpected unscheduled bleeding while on hormone therapy).
- **Required Action:** Schedule an appointment with your doctor promptly. While frequently caused by benign factors such as endometrial or vaginal atrophy or benign polyps, approximately 10% of cases are associated with endometrial hyperplasia or malignancy. Early assessment with transvaginal ultrasound or endometrial sampling is standard practice.

## Symptoms Warranting a Scheduled Consultation
- **Severe Disruption to Daily Life:** Hot flashes, chronic insomnia, or fatigue that impedes your ability to work, drive, or maintain daily activities.
- **Persistent Mood Changes:** New-onset severe anxiety, depressive episodes, panic symptoms, or feelings of despair that persist beyond temporary hormonal fluctuations.
- **Unmanaged Genitourinary Symptoms:** Chronic dyspareunia (painful sex), persistent vulvar burning, or recurrent urinary tract infections that do not respond to non-hormonal OTC moisturizers.
- **Early Age of Onset:** Experiencing menopause symptoms or irregular cycles before age 40 (Primary Ovarian Insufficiency / premature menopause) requires comprehensive specialist evaluation to protect bone, cognitive, and cardiovascular health.
