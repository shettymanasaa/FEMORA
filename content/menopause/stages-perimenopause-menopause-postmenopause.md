# The Menopause Transition: Perimenopause, Menopause, and Postmenopause

Menopause is a natural biological milestone that reflects the gradual transition of ovarian follicle activity and hormone production.

## The Three Stages

### 1. Perimenopause (The Transition)
- **Hormonal Dynamics:** As ovarian follicular reserve declines, follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) rises, causing erratic bursts of estrogen followed by sharp declines, and irregular or anovulatory cycles.
- **Timing:** Typically begins in a woman's 40s (average onset age 47) and lasts between 4 and 8 years.
- **Presenting Characteristics:** Changes in cycle frequency (cycles may shorten to 21 days or lengthen to 60+ days), heavier or lighter flow, sleep changes, hot flashes, and mood shifts.

### 2. Menopause (The Milestone)
- **Clinical Definition:** Diagnosed retrospectively after **12 consecutive months of amenorrhea** (absence of periods) without other pathological or physiological causes.
- **Timing:** The average age of natural menopause globally is 51 years.

### 3. Postmenopause (Long-Term Health)
- **Hormonal Dynamics:** Ovaries produce very low levels of estradiol, though the adrenal glands and peripheral adipose tissue continue to convert androgens into estrone.
- **Focus of Care:** Long-term cardiovascular protection, bone density preservation (osteopenia/osteoporosis prevention), and genitourinary health.
