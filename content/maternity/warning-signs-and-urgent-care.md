# Pregnancy and Postpartum Warning Signs: When to Seek Urgent Care

Certain symptoms indicate potentially serious maternal or fetal complications. Never hesitate to contact your maternity triage unit, midwife, obstetrician, or emergency services if you experience any of the red flags below.

## Immediate Emergency Red Flags (Call 911 / 999 or Go to Maternity Triage)

1. **Vaginal Bleeding:** Any bright red bleeding, heavy spotting, or passage of clots at any point during pregnancy.
2. **Symptoms of Preeclampsia or Severe Hypertension:**
   - A persistent, severe headache that does not respond to fluids or acetaminophen.
   - Visual changes such as aura, flashing lights, spots, or sudden blurriness.
   - Severe pain directly under the ribs or in the upper right side of your abdomen (epigastric pain).
   - Sudden, pronounced swelling in the face, hands, or around the eyes.
3. **Decreased or Altered Fetal Movement:**
   - Once regular movement is established (by 24–28 weeks), any noticeable reduction, slowing down, or cessation of fetal kicks. *Never wait until the next day to check on reduced movements.*
4. **Fluid Leakage / Suspected Ruptured Membranes:**
   - Continuous trickling or gushing of watery fluid from the vagina prior to 37 weeks, or fluid that is green, brown, or foul-smelling.
5. **Cardiopulmonary Signs:**
   - Sudden shortness of breath, severe chest tightness, coughing up blood, or sudden severe pain, swelling, and redness in one calf (suspicion of Deep Vein Thrombosis or Pulmonary Embolism).
6. **Signs of Systemic Infection:**
   - Maternal fever of 38°C (100.4°F) or higher, severe chills, foul-smelling vaginal discharge, or burning urination accompanied by back pain.
7. **Postpartum Hemorrhage Red Flags:**
   - Soaking through one or more large maternity pads an hour for two consecutive hours, or passing blood clots larger than a golf ball.
