# Postpartum Physical Recovery and Fourth Trimester Healing

The "fourth trimester" encompasses the 12 weeks following childbirth, during which the maternal body undergoes dramatic physiological rehabilitation.

## Physiological Milestones of Recovery
- **Uterine Involution and Afterpains:** The uterus contracts back to its pre-pregnancy size over 6 weeks. These contractions ("afterpains") can feel like menstrual cramps, especially during breastfeeding due to oxytocin release.
- **Lochia Progression:**
  - *Lochia Rubra (Days 1–4):* Dark or bright red discharge containing blood, decidua, and mucus.
  - *Lochia Serosa (Days 4–14):* Thinner, brownish-pink discharge.
  - *Lochia Alba (Weeks 2–6):* Creamy yellowish-white discharge gradually diminishing.
- **Perineal Healing:** If tearing or an episiotomy occurred, tissue generally heals within 2 to 3 weeks. Use peri-bottles with warm water, witch hazel pads, and ice packs to promote comfort.
- **Cesarean Incision Care:** Keep the incision clean and dry. Avoid lifting heavy objects (anything heavier than your newborn) for the first 4 to 6 weeks. Watch for wound redness, spreading warmth, or separation.
- **Pelvic Floor & Core Care:** Gentle pelvic floor contractions (Kegels) can be resumed when comfortable, helping restore bladder control and pelvic support.
