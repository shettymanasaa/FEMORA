# Common Pregnancy Symptoms and Comfort Measures

Pregnancy induces profound systemic physiological shifts across the cardiovascular, endocrine, and musculoskeletal systems.

## Frequent Symptoms and Management
- **Nausea and Morning Sickness:** Frequently peaks between weeks 6 and 12. Eating frequent, small carbohydrate-rich meals, staying hydrated between rather than during meals, and ginger tea or lozenges can alleviate symptoms.
- **Round Ligament Pain:** Sharp or dull aching twinges on either side of the lower abdomen caused by the stretching of ligaments supporting the growing uterus. Slow posture transitions, belly bands, and warm baths provide relief.
- **Heartburn and Acid Reflux:** Progesterone relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter, while the expanding uterus compresses gastric space. Elevating the head of your bed and avoiding spicy, fried, or large late-night meals help prevent reflux.
- **Braxton Hicks Contractions:** Mild, irregular, painless or slightly uncomfortable uterine tightening that does not increase in intensity or frequency and often resolves with hydration and resting.
- **Lower Extremity Edema:** Fluid accumulation in feet and ankles resulting from increased circulating blood volume and venous compression by the uterus. Elevating legs when seated and wearing graduated compression stockings improve circulation.
