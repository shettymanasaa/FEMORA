# Birth Preparation, Labor Stages, and Hospital Checklist

Planning for labor and delivery helps you and your birth partner feel organized and confident.

## The Three Stages of Labor
1. **First Stage (Cervical Dilation):**
   - *Latent Phase:* The cervix softens, thins (effaces), and dilates up to 4–5 cm. Contractions may be irregular or spaced 5 to 20 minutes apart.
   - *Active Phase:* Contractions become stronger, longer (lasting 60 seconds), and occur every 3 to 4 minutes. Dilation advances from 6 cm to 10 cm (full dilation).
2. **Second Stage (Birth of the Baby):**
   - Full cervical dilation to delivery. The mother expels the baby through coordinated contractions and maternal pushing efforts.
3. **Third Stage (Delivery of Placenta):**
   - The placenta and fetal membranes separate from the uterine wall and are delivered, usually within 5 to 30 minutes.

## Hospital Bag Checklist Essentials
- **Documents:** Antenatal care record/notes, photo identification, hospital registration cards, printed birth preferences.
- **For the Mother:**
  - 2–3 comfortable nightgowns or oversized tees suitable for breastfeeding and skin-to-skin contact.
  - 5–6 pairs of high-waisted cotton or disposable underwear.
  - 2 packs of absorbent postpartum/maternity sanitary pads.
  - Non-skid slippers, warm socks, lip balm, toiletry bag, and long phone charging cables.
  - Nursing bras and breast pads.
- **For the Baby:**
  - 3–4 newborn or 0-3 month sleepsuits (onesies) and vests.
  - Soft baby blanket, newborn scratch mittens, and hat.
  - 1 pack of newborn diapers and fragrance-free water wipes.
  - Approved rear-facing infant car seat properly installed for departure.
