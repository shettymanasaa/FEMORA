# Maternity Appointments and Preparing Questions for Your Clinician

> **Notice:** This document is currently marked as **Pending Clinical Review**. It provides draft suggestions to organize your questions before clinic visits.

## Structuring Your Doctor Appointments
Prenatal visits often move quickly. Preparing your symptom trends, questions, and medication list ahead of time ensures you cover what matters most to you.

## Suggested Questions by Trimester

### First Trimester Visits
1. Which prenatal vitamins and iron supplements do you recommend based on my blood tests?
2. What over-the-counter medications are approved for headaches or cold symptoms?
3. What is the emergency phone number for out-of-hours maternity triage?

### Second Trimester Visits
1. What should I expect during the mid-pregnancy anatomy scan?
2. How should I prepare for the oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) for gestational diabetes?
3. What are normal variations in fetal movements at this stage?

### Third Trimester Visits
1. When should I call the labor unit regarding contractions or waters breaking?
2. How does the hospital handle group B strep (GBS) screening and antibiotics?
3. What are your facility's protocols regarding delayed cord clamping and skin-to-skin contact?

### Postpartum Visits (2-Week & 6-Week Checks)
1. Has my perineum or cesarean incision healed sufficiently?
2. When is it safe for me to resume high-impact exercise or sexual intimacy?
3. What contraceptive options are compatible with my feeding plans?
